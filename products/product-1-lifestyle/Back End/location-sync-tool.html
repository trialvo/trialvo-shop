Step 1 — Sync into Staging Tables
🟡 Pathao Locations
Fetches all Pathao cities → zones → areas and fills pathao_locations_raw.
⚠ Takes 2–3 min due to rate-limit sleeps.
POST /api/v1/config/sync-pathao-locations
🔵 Steadfast Locations
Fetches all Steadfast districts + police stations and fills steadfast_locations_raw.
Usually completes in < 30 seconds.
POST /api/v1/config/sync-steadfast-locations
Step 2 — Merge into location_mappings

Run Dry-Run Preview first to see what will change without writing to the DB. Then run Apply Merge to commit.

POST /api/v1/config/merge-location-mappings
